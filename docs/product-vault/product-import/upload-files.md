# Upload files for each state

**Audience:** External

You upload files one state at a time.

![Import page for a state, with tabs and the Browse files button](../../assets/product-import/upload-files.webp)

## Upload your files

1. Open your product in the Vault.
2. Choose a state from the **Jurisdiction** selector.
3. Open the **Forms** tab and choose **Browse files**, or drag and drop. Forms accept DOC, DOCX or PDF.
4. Open the **Manual** tab and upload your manuals. Manuals accept PDF, DOC, DOCX, XLS or XLSX.
5. Open the **Supporting Materials** tab and upload your supporting documents.

!!! tip "Quick tips"
    Uploading one file at a time is the most reliable. If a tab count still shows 0 after an upload, refresh the page.

## Add metadata

New files show **Draft**, and some show **Metadata incomplete** until you add their details.

1. Choose the pencil icon on a file.
2. For a form, enter the **Form number** (for example, HO 0003) and the **Edition**.
3. Choose **Save metadata**.
