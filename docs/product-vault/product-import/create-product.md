# Create the product

1. Open **Product Vault** and choose **Add product**.
2. Enter a **Product name**.
3. Enter a **Description**.
4. Choose an **Insurance type**: **Personal** or **Commercial**.
5. Choose the **Lines of business**.
6. Choose your **Group**.
7. Choose your **Underwriting company**.
8. Select every state you need under **Jurisdictions**.
9. Choose **Add product**.

You see **Product created successfully**, and the product status shows **Pending Import**. Nice work!

## Lines of business

| Insurance type | Lines of business |
|---|---|
| Personal | Homeowners, Auto, Dwelling Property, Homeowners By Peril |
| Commercial | Builders Risk, Motor Truck Cargo, Inland Marine, Commercial Output |
