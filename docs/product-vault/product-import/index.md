# Product import

Watch this one-minute demo to see how a product goes into the **Product Vault** (the Vault), from a new product to extracted rules.

## The four steps

You can import a product in one sitting.

1. [Create the product](create-product.md). Give it a name, a line of business, and its states.
2. [Upload files for each state](upload-files.md). Add your forms, manuals, and supporting documents.
3. [Process and extract rules](process-and-extract-rules.md). The Vault reads your files and pulls out the rules.
4. Review and publish your first release. `TODO verify`

## Before you start

Have your files ready for each state, in three groups:

| Group | File types |
|---|---|
| Forms | Word or PDF |
| Manuals | Word or Excel |
| Supporting documents | PDF |

!!! tip "One-time setup"
    Your **Group** and **Underwriting Company** should already exist in **System Administration** → **Lists**. See [Before you start](../before-you-start.md).
