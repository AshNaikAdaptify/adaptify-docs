# Shared assets across states

When the same document is used in more than one place, for example the same form in Alabama and Alaska, Adaptify stores it once and marks it as shared. You can see the reuse at a glance.

## Spot a shared document

On a product's **Forms** or **Manual** tab, a shared document shows a **Shared** badge with a count, for example **Shared · 2**.

## Add metadata before you share

Add a document's metadata, such as its form number and edition, before you use it in another state. Once a document is shared, its **Edition** and title can lock. If that happens, contact Adaptify support.

## Update a shared form

You update a shared form in a Workbench project, like any other form. See [Update forms](../workbench/update-forms.md). Adaptify starts the change as a draft. The shared document and its unique key stay unchanged until you publish.
