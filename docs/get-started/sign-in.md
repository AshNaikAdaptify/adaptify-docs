# Sign in and Org Setup

You sign in with your email first, then your password. Everything you see after that (products, projects and filings) belongs to your organization.

## Sign in

1. Open the Adaptify sign-in page your organization uses. If you don't have the address, ask your organization admin.
2. On **Login to your account**, enter your email under **Email**, then select **Continue**.
3. Enter your **Password** and sign in. To check what you typed, select the eye icon in the field.
4. The **Dashboard** opens and greets you by name: "Welcome back".

Typed the wrong email? On the password step, select the arrow next to your email to go back and change it.

Forgot your password? Select **Forgot password?** on the password step and follow the instructions.

## Confirm your organization

1. Check that the **Dashboard** shows the products and projects you expect.
2. Your initials appear at the top right of every page.
3. If you're an organization admin, your organization's name is at the top of **System Administration** → **Account management**.

If you don't see your organization's products, contact your organization admin.
