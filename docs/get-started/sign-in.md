# Sign in and Org Setup

You sign in with your email first, then your password. Everything you see after that (products, projects and filings) belongs to your organization.

## Sign in

1. Open your Adaptify sign-in page. `TODO verify` the sign-in address for your organization.
2. On **Login to your account**, enter your email under **Email**, then select **Continue**.
3. Enter your **Password**. To check what you typed, select the eye icon in the field.
4. Select the button under the password to sign in. `TODO verify` the button label.
5. The **Dashboard** opens and greets you by name: "Welcome back".

Typed the wrong email? On the password step, select the arrow next to your email to go back and change it.

Forgot your password? Select **Forgot password?** on the password step. `TODO verify` the reset steps.
