# Your first 10 minutes: tour a product

Take a read-only tour of one product, one project and one filing. You only look, so nothing changes.

**Before you start:** you need a product with a published release. Parts 4 and 5 also need a project and a filing; skip them if you don't have any yet.

## 1. Open a product

1. In the left navigation, choose **Product Vault**.
2. In the **Product catalog**, open a product, for example **Personal Auto**.

## 2. Pick a state and a release

At the top of the product page:

- **Jurisdiction** picks the state.
- **Release** picks the version, for example **Baseline**.
- **Current release** marks the release in use. **New business** and **Renewal** show its dates.

## 3. Look through the tabs

The tabs hold the product's assets for that state and release: **Forms**, **Manual**, **Rates**, **Rules** and **Supporting Materials**. Each tab shows a count.

- On **Forms**, each row lists the form number and edition.
- **Shared · 2** marks a form used in two states.
- **Update** marks a form changed in that release.
- **Rules** lists the rules extracted from your manuals.

## 4. Open a project

1. In the left navigation, choose **Workbench**.
2. In the **Project portfolio**, open a project.

A combined project shows how many child projects are complete, for example **0 of 2 complete**, with the tabs **Projects**, **Notes**, **Team** and **Activity**. Open a child project to see which phase it's in.

## 5. Open a filing

1. In the left navigation, choose **Filing Manager**.
2. Open a filing card.

The filing header shows the **Jurisdiction**, **Filing type**, **Product**, **Target submission**, **Owner** and **Projects**, and a tracker shows how many steps are ready. Under the **Filing** tab, **Filing information** shows the status of the **Filing package** and the **Submission**, followed by the forms and rates in scope. The other tabs are **Objections**, **Notes**, **Team** and **Activity**.
