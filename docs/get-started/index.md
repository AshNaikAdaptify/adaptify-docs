# Get Started

Adaptify is an Insurance Product Lifecycle Management (IPLM) platform.

From concept through the operational life of a product, carriers manage the entire lifecycle in one platform.

## The platform

- **[Product Vault](../product-vault/index.md)** — Governed repository for all products. See also [Product import](../product-vault/product-import/index.md).
- **[Project Workbench](../workbench/index.md)** — Collaborative environment for building new products and updating existing ones.
- **[Rating Sandbox](../sandbox/index.md)** — Evaluate rates, test options, and opportunities to improve your offerings.
- **[Filing Manager](../filing-manager/index.md)** — Manage filings and objection handling all the way to approvals.

## Dashboard and Product Vault

- **[Dashboard](dashboard.md)** — Manage your actions and activities in an easy to use dashboard.
- **[Product Vault](../product-vault/index.md)** — Bring products into the vault. Start with [Product import](../product-vault/product-import/index.md).
