# Glossary

Terms you'll see in Adaptify and in these docs, in alphabetical order.

- **Admitted / Non-admitted:** The two ways to finish a Workbench project with **Complete project**. **Admitted** creates a filing for each state in Filing Manager. **Non-admitted** completes the project without a filing.
- **Archive project:** The last step on an approved filing. Choosing **Create new release** while archiving makes a new release of the product in the Vault.
- **Baseline:** The first release of a product's state, created when you publish it with **Review & publish**.
- **Child project:** One project inside a combined project, for one project type: **Forms**, **Rates** or **Rules**.
- **Combined project:** A **Product update** that covers more than one project type. It holds a child project for each type.
- **Current release:** The label on the release in use for a product's state.
- **Disposition status:** The state's decision on a filing's submission, for example **Pending** or **Approved**.
- **Filing:** The Filing Manager record for one state's submission, created when you complete a project as Admitted.
- **Filing package:** The ZIP of a filing's documents that you generate in Filing Manager and submit to the state through SERFF.
- **IPLM:** Insurance Product Lifecycle Management: managing a product from concept through its operational life in one place.
- **Jurisdiction:** A state the product is sold in. Use the **Jurisdiction** picker on a product page to switch states.
- **Manual:** The product page tab for rating and rules manuals. Adaptify extracts rules from them.
- **Objection:** A question or issue the state raises about a filing. You add and track objections and your responses on the filing's **Objections** tab.
- **Phase:** A stage in a Workbench project: **Select**, **Review**, **Approve** and **End**. Use **Submit** to move work to the next phase.
- **Prior approval:** An approval type you can choose when you complete a project as Admitted and when you record a submission.
- **Process documents:** The Vault action that turns uploaded files into product assets and extracts rules from your manuals.
- **Product update:** A Workbench project that updates the forms, rates or rules of a product that's already in the Vault.
- **Release:** A version of a product's assets for one state, for example **Baseline**. Use the **Release** picker on a product page to switch versions.
- **SERFF tracking #:** The tracking number from SERFF that you record on a filing's submission.
- **Shared:** A badge on a document used in more than one state, for example **Shared · 2**.
- **Supporting Materials:** The product page tab for supporting documents.
- **Update:** A badge on a form that changed in a release.
