# Add the product's documents

Every new product starts as an idea and takes shape in the Project Workbench. Your team builds its forms, rates and rules in Workbench projects, and they all come together in the product's release.

After you [start a new product project](start.md), add the product's forms to it. For each form, upload its file and give it a form number, an edition and a name. Each form you add is listed in the project as an addition. To add many forms at once, use **Bulk import**.

![The Forms tab with one form added, change type Addition](../../assets/workbench/new-product-project-form-added.webp)

## Rates and rules

A project has one type, so rates and rules each need their own project. Start it the same way as your forms project, but choose rates or rules as the project type, then add each rate or rule with its name and edition.

Rates and rules projects merge into the product's release. They don't create a separate product.
