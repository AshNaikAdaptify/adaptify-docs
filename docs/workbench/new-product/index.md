# Build a new product in Workbench

A **New product** project takes a brand-new insurance product from idea to build in the Project Workbench (the Workbench). Your team works on it together, phase by phase, like any other project. When you complete it, the product lands in the Product Vault with its first release.

## The steps

1. [Start a new product project](start.md). Choose **New product** and work through the project wizard.
2. [Add the product's documents](add-documents.md): add each form with its file. A project has one type, so rates and rules each need their own project (a **Rates** or **Rules** project).
3. [Finish and publish the new product](finish.md): move the project to **End** and complete it as **Non-admitted**. The product gets a **Baseline** release, marked **Current release**.

You won't see the product in the Product Vault until the project is completed. `TODO verify` whether that's intended.

To move the project between phases, see [Move work through phases](../phases.md).

## The other way: Add product in the Product Vault

You can also select **Add product** in the **Product Vault**. That creates the product right away with no Workbench project, marked **Pending Import**, with no release yet. You then upload, process and publish each state yourself. See [Create a product](../../product-vault/create-product.md).
