# Build a new product in Workbench

Every new product starts as an idea and takes shape in the Project Workbench. Your team builds its forms, rates and rules in Workbench projects, and they all come together in the product's release.

A **New product** project takes a brand-new insurance product from idea to build in the Project Workbench (the Workbench). Your team works on it together, phase by phase, like any other project. When you complete it, the product lands in the Product Vault with its first release.

## The steps

1. [Start a new product project](start.md): describe the product and set up the project and its team.
2. [Add the product's documents](add-documents.md): add the product's forms. Rates and rules each get their own project, and they merge into the same release.
3. [Finish and publish the new product](finish.md): move the project through its phases and complete it. The product then shows up in the Product Vault with its first release.

To move the project between phases, see [Move work through phases](../phases.md).

## The other way: Add product in the Product Vault

You can also select **Add product** in the **Product Vault**. That creates the product right away, with no Workbench project and no release yet. You then upload, process and publish each state yourself. See [Create a product](../../product-vault/create-product.md).
