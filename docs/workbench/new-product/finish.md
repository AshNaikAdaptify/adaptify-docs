# Finish and publish the new product

Every new product starts as an idea and takes shape in the Project Workbench. Your team builds its forms, rates and rules in Workbench projects, and they all come together in the product's release.

When the product's documents are in, your team moves the project through its phases, from **Select** to **End**. At each phase, someone reviews the work and passes it on with notes, and from **Review** on they can send it back. See [Move work through phases](../phases.md).

At **End**, complete the project as **Non-admitted**, with the product's state and effective dates. The product then appears in the **Product Vault** with its first release, **Baseline**, marked **Current release**, with your forms in place. You don't need **Review & publish**. Before you confirm, the preview shows the product's state, its new **Baseline** release and each form marked **New**.

From then on, the product changes like any other published product. See [Release to New Release](../../release-to-new-release/index.md).
