# Start a new product project

Every new product starts as an idea and takes shape in the Project Workbench. Your team builds its forms, rates and rules in Workbench projects, and they all come together in the product's release.

Start here when your team is building a brand-new insurance product. In **Workbench**, select **+ New project** and choose **New product**. A short wizard walks you through the setup:

- **Choose what you're building.** Pick a new product and the project type: forms, rates or rules. A project has one type, so rates and rules each get their own project. (Adding a state to a product you already have? See [Start a project to add a state](../create-project/new-jurisdiction.md).)
- **Describe the product.** Give it a name and description, and say who it's for: the group, underwriting company, line of business, state and type of insurance.
- **Set up the project.** Give the project a title, a description and a due date, and add your teammates.

When you create the project, it opens at its first phase, ready for the product's documents.

![The new project right after it's created, with an empty Forms tab](../../assets/workbench/new-product-project-empty.webp)

If you can't pick an underwriting company for your group, ask your organization admin to set one up.
