# Update forms

Use a form project to give one or more forms a new edition. If your project is combined, first open its forms child project from the **Projects** tab.

## Steps

1. Open the project. Its **Forms** tab lists the forms in the project.
2. Choose **Add form**. The **Add form changes** dialog opens.
3. Under **Change type**, choose **Update form**, then choose **Next**.
4. Select the forms to update, then choose **Next**.
5. Enter the **Updated edition** when the dialog asks for it, for example 01 27.
6. Check the **Confirm** step, then choose **Change**.

The forms appear on the **Forms** tab with their new edition. When the work is done, use **Submit** to move the project to its next phase.

!!! note "Shared forms"
    If a form is shared by other products or states, the update starts as a draft. The shared document and its unique key stay unchanged until you publish.
