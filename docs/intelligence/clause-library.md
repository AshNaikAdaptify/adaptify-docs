# Clause Library

The **Clause Library** is where you browse clauses and place them in a section hierarchy, so you can find and reuse standard wording.

## Find a clause

1. In the left navigation, under **Intelligence**, open **Clause Library**.
2. Check the summary cards: **Clauses**, **Jurisdictions**, **Lines of business** and **Associated forms**.
3. Search with **Search clauses by name or clause text**.
4. Narrow the list with **Lines of business**, or with **Placement** (**All**, **Placed** or **Unplaced**).

If nothing matches, you see "No clause found". Try another keyword or line of business.

To add a clause yourself, select **+ New clause**.

## Extract clauses from your forms

Clauses come from your published forms. The **Forms to process** card shows how many forms are waiting.

1. On the **Forms to process** card, select **Start**. The **Clause extraction** page opens.
2. Find your product with **Find a product or state**, then select **Process all** for it (the button shows the number of forms).
3. Adaptify parses each form. You review each one in Word, then the reviewed forms are extracted together.

The page tracks each form as **Not started**, **Skipped**, **Parsing**, **To review**, **Reviewed** or **Extracted**.

When new editions of your forms come in, the **New editions** card shows them. Select **Open** to see them.
