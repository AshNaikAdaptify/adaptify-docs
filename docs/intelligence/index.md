# Clause, statute and objection libraries

The **Intelligence** group in the left navigation holds three libraries you can search while you build products and answer filings.

| Library | Use it to | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Clause Library** | Browse the clauses in your forms, and start clause extraction from your published forms. | [Clause Library](clause-library.md) |
| **Statute Library** | Look up the statutes behind the compliance check, by state. | [Statute Library](statute-library.md) |
| **Objection Library** | Search past SERFF objections to prepare your responses. | [Objection Library](objection-library.md) |
