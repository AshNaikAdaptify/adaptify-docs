# Clause, statute and objection libraries

The **Intelligence** group in the left navigation holds three libraries you can search while you build products and answer filings.

| Library | Use it to | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Clause Library** | Browse the clauses in your forms, and start clause extraction from your published forms. | [Clause Library](clause-library.md) |
| **Statute Library** | Look up the statutes behind the compliance check, by state. | [Statute Library](statute-library.md) |
| **Objection Library** | Search past SERFF objections to prepare your responses. | [Objection Library](objection-library.md) |

## Which library when

| You're | Open |
|---|---|
| Drafting or reviewing a form and want standard wording | **Clause Library**: search clauses by name or text, or narrow by line of business. |
| Checking which state rules apply to a type of insurance | **Statute Library**: pick a state, then narrow by **TOI scope**. |
| Answering a state's objection on a filing | **Objection Library**: see how similar filings were questioned before. Then log your response on the filing's **Objections** tab. |

## Good to know

- Clauses come from your published forms through clause extraction. The **Forms to process** card shows how many forms are waiting. See [Extract clauses from your forms](clause-library.md#extract-clauses-from-your-forms).
- Statute Library rows are tagged by type of insurance and severity. Choose a state first, because **TOI scope** stays empty until you do.
