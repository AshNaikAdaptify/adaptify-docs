# Objection Library

The Objection Library holds past SERFF objections that states have raised. Look through it to see how a state has questioned similar filings before, and to prepare your response. It's read-only: you look things up and nothing changes.

## Find an objection

1. In the left navigation, under **Intelligence**, choose **Objection Library**.
2. Look for objections like the one you're answering, and open one to read it.

## When to use it

- **A state sends an objection letter:** before you write your response, see how the state has questioned similar filings.
- **You're preparing a filing:** check what the state has objected to before.

## Next

To answer an objection on one of your filings, add it to the filing's **Objections** tab. See [Track objections and responses](../release-to-new-release/handle-objections.md).
