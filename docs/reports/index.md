# Reports

Reports gives you one view of Product Vault currentness, Project Workbench execution and Filing Manager progress across your products. Open it from **Reports** in the left navigation.

## Choose a period

At the top of the **Reports dashboard**, pick the **Period**, for example **Current quarter**. The **Range** and **As of** dates show what the figures cover.

## Product Vault tab

- **Summary cards:** **Products imported**, **Products refreshed**, **Effective this period**, **Total jurisdictions**, **Current**, **Needs refresh** and **Critical**.
- **Product availability & currentness matrix:** each product by jurisdiction, coloured by how recently it was updated.
- **Product inventory:** each product with its **States**, **Last import**, **Last refresh**, **Effective date** and **Status**, for example **Needs refresh**.
- **Threshold setup:** the day limits behind the colours, under **Product currentness** and **LOB status**. Choose **Edit** to change them.
- **Product R/Y/G rules:** what **Green**, **Yellow**, **Red** and **Gray** mean, for jurisdictions and for products.

## Project Workbench and Filing Manager tabs

The other tabs cover your Workbench projects and your filings.
